# Aadhaar Card Official Procedures and Guidelines
Document Title: UIDAI Official Guidance on Lost Aadhaar, Download & Corrections
Category: Aadhaar Services
Authority: Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI)

## Section 1: Procedure for Lost Aadhaar Card
If a citizen loses their Aadhaar card, they can retrieve it using their Aadhaar number (UID) or Enrolment ID (EID).

### Steps to Retrieve Lost Aadhaar Online:
1. Visit the official UIDAI portal (myAadhaar at myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in).
2. Click on the "Retrieve EID / Aadhaar number" service option.
3. Select whether you want to retrieve your 12-digit Aadhaar Number (UID) or 28-digit Enrolment ID (EID).
4. Enter your full name as printed on your Aadhaar card and registered Mobile Number or registered Email ID.
5. Click "Send OTP". Enter the 6-digit OTP received on your registered mobile number.
6. Upon successful verification, your Aadhaar number or EID will be sent directly to your registered mobile number via SMS.

## Section 2: How to Download E-Aadhaar Online
E-Aadhaar is a legally valid, digitally signed electronic document equivalent to the physical Aadhaar card under Section 4(3) of the Aadhaar Act 2016.

### Steps to Download E-Aadhaar:
1. Go to myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in and click "Download Aadhaar".
2. Select your ID type: Aadhaar Number (12 digits), Enrolment ID (28 digits), or Virtual ID (VID, 16 digits).
3. Enter the ID details and security captcha.
4. Click "Send OTP" and enter the OTP received on your mobile.
5. You can choose a "Masked Aadhaar" if you wish to hide the first 8 digits of your Aadhaar number for privacy.
6. Click "Verify & Download". The downloaded file is a password-protected PDF.
7. The password to open your e-Aadhaar PDF consists of the FIRST 4 LETTERS of your name in CAPITAL letters followed by your YEAR OF BIRTH (YYYY). Example: If name is ANISH KUMAR and birth year is 1989, password is `ANIS1989`.

## Section 3: Ordering a Physical Aadhaar PVC Card
Citizens who prefer a physical card can order a durable Aadhaar PVC card online.

### Process for Aadhaar PVC Card:
1. Visit myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in and click "Order Aadhaar PVC Card".
2. Enter your 12-digit Aadhaar number or 28-digit Enrolment ID.
3. If your mobile number is not registered, check "My Mobile number is not registered" and enter any non-registered mobile number to receive OTP.
4. Pay a nominal fee of Rs. 50 (inclusive of GST and Speed Post delivery charges).
5. Note down the Service Request Number (SRN) to track the dispatch status.
6. The PVC Aadhaar card will be delivered to your registered address via Speed Post within 5-7 working days.

## Section 4: Aadhaar Update and Demographic Correction Procedure
If there are errors in your Aadhaar details (Name, Address, Date of Birth, Gender, or Mobile Number):

### Online Update (Address Only):
1. Citizens can update their address online by uploading valid address proof (Electricity bill, Passport, Bank Statement, Water Bill, etc.).
2. Visit myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in, log in using Aadhaar + OTP, and select "Update Address".
3. Upload scanned copy of proof document and pay Rs. 50 fee.

### Offline Update (Biometrics, Name, DOB, Mobile Number):
1. For biometric updates (Fingerprints, Iris, Photograph) or changes to Name, Date of Birth, Gender, and Mobile Number, you MUST visit a nearest Aadhaar Seva Kendra (ASK) or authorized bank/post office enrolment center.
2. Fill out the Aadhaar Enrolment/Correction Form.
3. Provide original supporting documents for verification (e.g., PAN card/Passport for Name proof, Birth Certificate for DOB proof).
4. No documents are required for Mobile Number or Email update; biometric authentication is required.
