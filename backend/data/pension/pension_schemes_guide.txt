# National Pension Schemes & Senior Citizen Assistance Guidelines
Document Title: Official Handbook on Pension Schemes, Eligibility & Application Procedures
Category: Pension Services
Authority: Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment & Department of Pensions

## Section 1: Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS)
The Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme provides monthly financial assistance to senior citizens belonging to Below Poverty Line (BPL) households.

### Eligibility Criteria:
1. The applicant must be a citizen of India.
2. Age: 60 years or above.
3. Financial status: Must belong to a household identified as Below Poverty Line (BPL) as per central government guidelines.

### Benefit Amount:
* For beneficiaries aged 60 to 79 years: Rs. 200 to Rs. 500 per month (Central + State share combined).
* For beneficiaries aged 80 years and above: Rs. 500 to Rs. 1000 per month depending on state implementation.

## Section 2: Required Documents for Pension Application
To apply for an Old Age Pension, Disability Pension, or Widow Pension, the following documents are mandatory:

1. **Proof of Age**: Birth Certificate, School Leaving Certificate, PAN Card, Voter ID, or Medical Officer Certificate confirming age.
2. **Proof of Residence / Address**: Aadhaar Card, Ration Card, Voter ID, or Utility Bill.
3. **Income / BPL Certificate**: BPL Ration Card, Income Certificate issued by Tehsildar or competent Revenue Officer.
4. **Bank Account Details**: Copy of active Bank Passbook (Aadhaar-seeded bank account for DBT direct benefit transfer).
5. **Passport Size Photographs**: 2 recent passport-size photographs of the applicant.
6. **Disability Certificate** (Only for Disability Pension): Certificate showing minimum 40% disability certified by a government medical board.
7. **Death Certificate of Husband** (Only for Widow Pension): Official death certificate issued by Registrar of Births & Deaths.

## Section 3: Step-by-Step Pension Application Procedure
Citizens can apply either online through state social welfare portals or offline via local administrative offices.

### Offline Application Method:
1. Visit the local Gram Panchayat office (in rural areas) or Block Development Office (BDO) / District Social Welfare Office (DSWO) (in urban areas).
2. Obtain the prescribed Pension Application Form free of cost or download it from the state welfare portal.
3. Fill in applicant personal details, address, BPL card number, and bank account number carefully.
4. Attach self-attested photocopies of all required documents (Aadhaar, BPL card, Bank Passbook, Age proof).
5. Submit the application form to the Social Welfare Officer / BDO office. Obtain an acknowledgment receipt.
6. The application undergoes physical verification by the Revenue Inspector/Patwari within 30 to 45 working days.
7. Upon sanction, pension money is credited directly into the applicant's bank account via Aadhaar Enabled Payment System (AEPS) or Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

## Section 4: National Pension System (NPS) for General Citizens
NPS is a voluntary, defined contribution retirement savings scheme open to all Indian citizens aged 18 to 70 years.

### How to Apply for NPS:
1. Citizens can open an NPS account online via eNPS portal (enps.nsdl.com) using Aadhaar or PAN.
2. Select Point of Presence (POP) bank.
3. Submit KYC verification documents online.
4. Obtain a 12-digit Permanent Retirement Account Number (PRAN).
