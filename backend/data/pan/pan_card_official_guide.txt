# PAN Card Issuance, Duplicate PAN & Correction Rules
Document Title: Official Income Tax Department Guidelines on PAN Services
Category: PAN Services
Authority: Income Tax Department, Government of India & NSDL/UTIITSL

## Section 1: Procedure for Lost PAN Card / Duplicate PAN Application
If a taxpayer loses their physical PAN card or it gets damaged/stolen, they can apply for a duplicate PAN card with the SAME PAN number.

### How to Apply for Duplicate PAN Card Online:
1. Visit the official NSDL (Protean) e-Gov portal (onlineservices.tin.egovnsdl.com) or UTIITSL portal (pan.utiitsl.com).
2. Select Application Type: **"Changes or Correction in existing PAN Data / Reprint of PAN Card (No changes in existing PAN data)"**.
3. Fill in basic personal details: Name, Date of Birth, Email ID, Mobile Number, and existing 10-digit PAN Number.
4. Complete e-KYC using Aadhaar OTP authentication.
5. Upload supporting documents:
   * Proof of Identity (Aadhaar / Voter ID / Passport)
   * Proof of Address (Aadhaar / Utility Bill)
   * Proof of PAN (Copy of old PAN card or PAN Allotment Letter; FIR copy if lost)
6. Pay prescribed fee: Rs. 50 for dispatch within India, or Rs. 959 for international delivery.
7. Submit application. A 15-digit Acknowledgement Number is generated to track status.
8. Physical duplicate PAN card will be delivered to registered address within 10-15 business days.

## Section 2: How to Download Instant e-PAN Card
Citizens needing urgent PAN verification can download digital e-PAN (legally valid under IT rules).

### Steps for Instant e-PAN Download:
1. Visit the Income Tax e-Filing portal (incometax.gov.in).
2. Go to "Instant e-PAN" section.
3. Click "Check Status / Download PAN".
4. Enter 12-digit Aadhaar number and captcha.
5. Enter OTP received on Aadhaar-linked mobile number.
6. View and Download e-PAN PDF instantly free of cost.
7. Password to open e-PAN PDF is Date of Birth in DDMMYYYY format (e.g. 05081995 for 5th August 1995).

## Section 3: PAN Card Correction and Data Update
To correct spelling errors in Name, Father's Name, or update Date of Birth / Address:
1. Fill "Request for New PAN Card Or/And Changes Or Correction in PAN Data" form online.
2. Select checkboxes next to fields requiring modification.
3. Upload supporting valid proof (e.g., Gazette Notification for name change, Marriage Certificate, Passport, Birth Certificate).
4. Authenticate via Aadhaar e-Sign or physically post signed acknowledgement form to NSDL/UTIITSL Pune/Mumbai office.
