# Flagship Government Schemes: PM-Kisan, Ayushman Bharat & Eligibility Guidelines
Document Title: Official Guidelines on Major Central & State Citizen Welfare Schemes
Category: Government Schemes
Authority: Ministry of Agriculture & Ministry of Health and Family Welfare

## Section 1: Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)
PM-KISAN is a Central Sector Scheme that provides income support to landholding farmer families across India.

### Benefits:
* Financial benefit of Rs. 6,000 per year transferred directly in three equal quarterly installments of Rs. 2,000 each into Aadhaar-seeded bank accounts.

### Eligibility Criteria:
1. Small and marginal landholder farmer families owning cultivable land up to 2 hectares in their name.
2. Mandatory e-KYC verification using Aadhaar OTP or biometric authentication at CSC centers.

### Exclusions:
* Institutional landholders, serving/retired government employees, income tax payers in last assessment year, doctors, engineers, lawyers, and monthly pensioners drawing Rs. 10,000 or more.

### Required Documents:
* Cultivable Land Holding Ownership Record (Khasra / Khatauni / Jamabandi).
* Aadhaar Card.
* Active Bank Account Passbook (Aadhaar Seeded).
* Mobile Number.

### How to Apply:
1. Self-registration on PM-KISAN portal (pmkisan.gov.in) under "Farmers Corner" -> "New Farmer Registration".
2. Enter Aadhaar number, state, district, land registration ID, and bank details.
3. Applications are verified by State Nodal Officers before sanction.

## Section 2: Ayushman Bharat - Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY)
PM-JAY is the world's largest health insurance scheme fully financed by the government.

### Benefits:
* Health coverage of Rs. 5 Lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization across empanelled public and private hospitals.
* Covers pre-hospitalization (3 days) and post-hospitalization (15 days) expenses including diagnostic tests and medicines.
* No restriction on family size, age, or gender.

### Eligibility:
* Families identified based on SECC 2011 (Social Economic Caste Census) criteria in rural and urban areas.
* Senior Citizens aged 70 years and above (Ayushman Vaya Vandana Card) regardless of income.

### How to Apply & Download Ayushman Card:
1. Visit beneficiary.nha.gov.in or download Ayushman App.
2. Enter Mobile Number and receive OTP.
3. Search by Scheme Name (PMJAY), State, District, and Aadhaar Number or Family ID / Ration Card Number.
4. Complete e-KYC via Aadhaar OTP or Face Authentication.
5. Download instant Ayushman Card PDF. Present card at hospital Helpdesk (Ayushman Mitra) for cashless treatment.
